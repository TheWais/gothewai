/Projects

What we build

Three systems that show how we take one concrete problem end to end.

/01 — HR · Operations

Internal requests agent

The problem: employee requests, leaves and questions pile up across email and chats, while the admin team loses hours sorting and chasing paperwork.

The solution: an agent that receives each request, classifies it, checks the documentation, queries internal systems and routes it to the right workflow. People keep making the decisions; the agent runs the front desk, around the clock.

WhatsApp & email · Internal systems · Human oversight

/02 — Sales · Marketing

B2B demand engine

The problem: manual prospecting — cold lists, little personalization and follow-up that depends on each salesperson's memory.

The solution: a system that identifies target companies, enriches the data, personalizes outreach and sustains follow-up, measuring every step. Your sales team walks into the conversation with the homework already done.

Targeting · Enrichment · Measured follow-up

/03 — Content · Media

Content production line

The problem: hours of recorded material that never become publishable pieces, because editing, subtitling and publishing eat up the team.

The solution: a pipeline that detects the best segments, edits them, subtitles them and leaves them ready to publish. From raw footage to a steady stream of pieces, with human editorial control.

Video · Automated editing · Subtitling
